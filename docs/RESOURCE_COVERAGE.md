# Resource coverage of LFE5U 85F

Field by field, every Trellis setting against the emulator is in
[SETTINGS_COVERAGE.md](SETTINGS_COVERAGE.md).

The main code has the [first DSP profiles](DSP_OUTPUT.md), [configuration preparation and a separate storage](CONFIGURATION_STATE.md), the [event kernel](EVENT_KERNEL.md), an [automatic plan from CRAM](AUTOMATIC_EXECUTION.md), [LUTRAM/RAMW](LUTRAM_EXECUTION.md), [global controls with explicit wake phases](SYSTEM_EXECUTION.md), and [EBR](EBR_EXECUTION.md). `emulate` ties ASYNC and qualified HY01 SYNC GSR to the FFs/EBR and the active DSP stages, GOE to the PIO, and GWDIS to memory writes. The full PLC volume includes all 83 640 FFs, the active RAMW, and EBR. DI of FF C in RAMW explicitly stays X. The physical POR/START/transport and the remaining families need further work; there are no fully closed families.

The latest collection is summarized in [RESEARCH_STATUS.md](RESEARCH_STATUS.md): 78 open topics, 37 descriptions of future experiment series, no hardware results. The new [implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) takes these limits into account. Research coverage is not counted as readiness of the executable models.

The coverage goal is all architecture instances, internal nodes, and available modes of the target chip. The table records the state of the inventory, decoding, and implementation. The presence of data in Trellis, of a structural object, of a mode decoder, and of verified behavior is assessed separately.

`Catalog` means that the settings or references related to the family have been extracted. `Prototype` means a partial implementation with limited checks. `Research` means that the structure, or the mapping of source records to physical nodes, still needs clarification. There are no fully closed families yet.

The full names and variants of all discovered settings are in [settings.csv](inventory/LFE5U-85F/settings.csv); the bit groups and connections are in [device_inventory.json](inventory/LFE5U-85F/device_inventory.json). The table below groups the work by family; each variant must get its own check at implementation.

The binding of the main instances and the statuses of all source references are described in [RESOURCE_BINDINGS.md](RESOURCE_BINDINGS.md). The first draft of the kernel architecture is in [STRUCTURAL_MODEL.md](STRUCTURAL_MODEL.md). The presence of a record in these catalogs does not mean implemented behavior.

## Family closure conditions

1. All instances and internal architecture nodes are represented by permanent objects bound to the physical map.
2. The effective settings are reconstructed from the corresponding CRAM regions, and the sharing of bits is explained.
3. For the modes, the state, outputs, event rules, and the relation to neighboring resources are defined.
4. The main modes and their interaction are verified; the limits of the reference and of the hardware checks are stated next to the result.
5. Unexplored modes and states remain open items and are not replaced by silent constants.

Disputed behavior rules are sent to the [hardware check queue](HARDWARE_VALIDATION.md). This allows the collection for the other families to continue while keeping the distinction between verified connections, synthesis assumptions, and observation on the chip.

Research IDs and relations are defined for the main instances; the field map covers all EBR/MULT18/ALU54 and the described clock/system resources. The first [decoder of whole fields and the INIT binding](CONFIG_DECODER.md) work; [EBR addressing](EBR_ADDRESSING.md) was researched separately from future execution. The exact EBR transitions and the owners of shared DSP registers still need checking. Service uncertainties are kept separately.
