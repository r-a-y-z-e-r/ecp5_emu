## Not used

- Blender's built-in text font (`<builtin>`, "Bfont Regular"): its glyphs differ from
  every font Blender's licence file lists, so its licence could not be established.
  The model sets its markings in Inter instead.
- The vendor 3D models (`.wrl`/`.step`) referenced by the ULX3S footprints: their
  licences are not stated in the design. Part bodies are modelled from datasheet
  dimensions instead.
- The radiona.org/ulx3s product photo: no licence stated. It was only looked at to
  fit the QA camera and compare colours; it is not in the repository.
